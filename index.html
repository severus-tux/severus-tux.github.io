Hi 👋 I'm Vishwa Prakash. I'm a PhD student in Computer science at Chennai Mathematical Institute. I'm working under the supervision of Prof. Prajakta Nimbhorkar. My PhD is supported by the TCS RSP Fellowship.

I am broadly interested in Computational Social Choice and Algorithmic Game Theory. I like working on anything that involves graph theory and combinatorics. 🧩

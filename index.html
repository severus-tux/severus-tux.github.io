Hi 👋 I'm Vishwa Prakash. I am a postdoctoral fellow at Keio University, Tokyo, hosted by Prof. Akihisa Tamura as a member of the ERATO Kojima Market Design Project. I am also a visiting researcher at the University of Tokyo, where I work with Prof. Ayumi Igarashi. My research interests are in discrete fair division, computational social choice, and algorithmic game theory.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Chennai Mathematical Institute, where I was advised by Prof. Prajakta Nimbhorkar. My PhD was supported by the TCS RSP Fellowship. Before joining Keio, I briefly worked as a research associate at TIFR with Prof. Umang Bhaskar.
